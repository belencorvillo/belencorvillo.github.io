Machine Learning in PLC Control

Designed and deployed a Deep Reinforcement Learning (PPO) agent to autonomously optimize the kinematics of an industrial Pick & Place cell.

  • IT/OT Bridge: Real-time communication between asynchronous Python training threads and the industrial PLC via OPC UA.
  • Continuous Multi-Variable Control: Custom Gymnasium environment mapping neural network decisions into 5 real-world kinematic parameters (velocity, acceleration, jerk, dynamic wait positions, and blending radius).
  • Autonomous Convergence: Multi-criteria reward function (balancing cycle time and energy consumption) converging to a consolidated, highly efficient strategy in 15 hours of training—verified via TensorBoard.

TARANTULA

Meet TARANTULA, my undergraduate thesis: a custom-engineered quadruped robot designed for complex inspection in confined and hazardous spaces.

Months of R&D went into bringing this robot to life. On the hardware side, it was designed in Autodesk Fusion, resulting in a machined carbon fiber and ASA polymer structure keeping the total mass strictly under 7 kg. The design is fully vertically reversible, meaning it can operate seamlessly even if flipped upside down.

On the software and electronics side, I formulated the mathematical framework with real-time 6-DOF inverse kinematics running on a custom C++ architecture. To interact robustly with its environment, I implemented active impedance control for dynamic impact absorption, programmed two locomotion gaits, and developed a custom-engineered PCB integrating CAN bus telemetry and Hall-effect sensors for tactile ground detection.

Balbo's Chess: Object-Oriented C++ Game Engine

Developed alongside four peers during my engineering degree, Balbo Chess is an interactive 2D game engine programmed in C++ (Visual Studio) and rendered with freeglut / OpenGL. Built on Object-Oriented Programming principles (inheritance and polymorphism), it implements the strategic Balbo's Chess variant on a custom diamond board with pieces themed as rival UPM engineering faculties.

For solo play against the machine, we integrated an autonomous AI engine powered by the Minimax algorithm with Alpha-Beta pruning and variable search depth, balancing deep tactical calculation with instantaneous response.
